Research

My doctoral research examines whether generic patient-reported outcome (PRO) instruments adequately capture the self-management competencies targeted by patient education programmes in chronic disease management. Using a scoping review methodology (JBI/PRISMA-ScR), I map 51 instruments against a 10-domain heuristic taxonomy grounded in WHO (1998) frameworks and Lorig's self-management model.

This work is part of my doctoral research on the assessment of the impact of patient education (PE) in people with rare diseases.
