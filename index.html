Bridging clinical experience, patient-reported outcomes and public health policy.

Explore my research Contact me

Research focus

Three complementary areas

My work connects the patient's perspective with the evidence decision-makers need from how outcomes are measured to how programmes are evaluated and funded.

01

Patient-Reported Outcomes (PROMs)

Critical analysis of measurement instruments — their content, coverage and psychometric qualities — with a particular focus on self-efficacy and what patients themselves consider meaningful.

  • Instrument analysis
  • Self-efficacy
02

Patient Education & Self-Management

Evaluating the competencies developed through therapeutic patient education (TPE / ETP) programmes, and how self-management skills can be defined, observed and measured.

  • TPE / ETP
  • Competency assessment
03

Health Programme Evaluation & Policy

Improving evaluation methods so that the evidence produced on health programmes is robust, comparable and genuinely useful for public decision-making.

  • Evaluation methods
  • Health policy

About me

Cira Ba

PhD Candidate · Former RN

Nancy, France

From clinical practice to research for public health

After ten years as a registered nurse, I saw first-hand how much patients' own experience of their condition shapes their care — and how rarely it is captured in the data used to steer health systems. My doctoral thesis at the Université de Lorraine examines how patient-reported outcomes and self-management competencies can be measured rigorously, so that patient education programmes are evaluated on what truly matters to patients.

  • PhD Candidate — Université de Lorraine
  • 10 years clinical experience — former RN
  • Specialised in PROMs & PE — patient education

Featured communication

Exploring the coverage of generic patient-reported outcome measures: a scoping review

EPH 2026 – European Public Health Conference Bilbao, Spain Poster presented

Cira Ba1, Jonathan Epstein1, Laetitia Ricci1, Francis Guillemin1

1Université de Lorraine

This scoping review maps the content of generic PROMs to identify which dimensions of health and self-management they actually capture and which remain under-represented. The findings inform the choice of instruments for evaluating patient education programmes and for embedding the patient's voice in health-system monitoring.

  • Measurement
  • Self-management
